Age-Appropriate Classrooms

From first smiles to kindergarten readiness, each classroom is designed around how children learn best at every stage.

🍼 6 weeks – 12 months

Infants

Gentle routines, sensory play, and lots of loving one-on-one care.

🚼 1 – 2 years

Toddlers

Exploration-driven activities that build language, motor skills, and independence.

🎨 3 – 4 years

Preschool

Early literacy, math concepts, creative arts, and social-emotional growth.

Pre-K

A kindergarten-readiness curriculum focused on confidence and school skills.
